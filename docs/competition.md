# Competitive context

All Items is being developed in a category with established private inventory tools such as Part-DB, Homebox, InvenTree, Shelf.nu, PartsBox, and Binner; AI-assisted inventory concepts such as Hoardo and SellRaze; and resource-sharing services such as Peerby, Yoodlize, ShareGrid, and Fat Llama.

The intended long-term direction combines a universal private inventory with optional object understanding, practical reuse discovery, and explicitly voluntary resource sharing. This describes the product direction, not a claim of uniqueness or a comparative evaluation. M0 is only a local item list and does not implement AI or sharing.
