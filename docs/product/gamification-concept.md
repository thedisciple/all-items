# Achievements and guided discovery concept

**Status: future product concept; no M2B implementation.** Do not add achievement tables, badges, ranking, telemetry, or achievement UI during M2B.

## User value

Optional recognition may help a user notice useful organizing actions and discover features. The goal is an organized, findable inventory, not more time in the app or more records for their own sake.

## Candidate local achievements

- **Inventory milestones:** First Item; progressive Collector levels; a user-defined workshop/home organization goal.
- **Data quality:** Photographer based on actual photo coverage; Everything in Its Place based on known locations; No More Mystery based on resolved unknown status; Detail Master based on useful metadata.
- **Product discovery:** Collection Explorer, Find It Fast, Multi-Location, and Backup Ready only after those behaviors exist and can be verified.
- **Practical reuse:** Maker or Second Life only after users can explicitly record project use; lending/exchange achievements only after such workflows exist.

## Levels and progress

Potential tiers may have a progress bar and a factual next target (for example, "30 of 50 items"). Example thresholds in the M2B brief (1, 5, 20, 50, 100) are hypotheses, not approved requirements. Prefer completion ratios for quality measures over raw volume. Define inclusion/exclusion rules, handle deletions, and avoid hidden composite scores.

## Ethics and privacy

- Entirely optional; provide a clear opt-out and an achievement-free core product.
- No streaks, artificial deadlines/scarcity, guilt prompts, purchase rewards, pay-to-progress, or incentives to publish property.
- No false percentile. Any later community comparison requires opt-in, a disclosed metric, sufficiently large and comparable cohorts, privacy review, and no raw inventory, names, locations, prices, or photos sent to a comparison service.
- Start with local calculations. Store only what is necessary; derive progress from existing data where possible.
- Use original All Items visual language; no monobank cat or other protected assets.

## Dependencies and decision gates

Before implementation: user research should show that recognition helps organization; a metric definition and deletion behavior must be reviewed; optional settings and privacy implications must be designed. Community comparisons require a real multi-user platform, consent, an adequate cohort, and a separate security/privacy assessment. Suggested horizon: M3 or later for local goals; community comparisons only after multi-user support. Complexity: medium (local), high (community). Status: idea.
